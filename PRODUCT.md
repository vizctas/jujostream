# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players using phones, TVs, streaming boxes, and desktop clients to browse and
launch games from a registered PC. The primary workflow must remain usable with
a gamepad and understandable without streaming expertise.

## Product Purpose

JUJO Stream turns a remote PC game library into a responsive console-style
experience. Success means fast discovery and pairing, attractive game browsing,
and reliable launch and streaming without exposing infrastructure complexity.

## Brand Personality

Premium, cinematic, and approachable. The interface should feel deliberate and
impactful while remaining lightweight during navigation.

## Anti-references

- Pixelated portrait covers enlarged as landscape artwork.
- A detached poster floating over a heavily blurred duplicate.
- Decorative effects that obscure the selected game's artwork.
- Dense administration UI or unfamiliar controls in the play flow.

## Design Principles

- Let official game artwork create the cinematic impact.
- Preserve familiar console navigation and immediate gamepad feedback.
- Improve source quality without unexpectedly redesigning established layouts.
- Prefer graceful degradation over misleading or malformed artwork.
- Keep motion subtle, smooth, and subordinate to navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect the existing reduced-effects setting, maintain readable overlays on
variable artwork, preserve keyboard and gamepad parity, and avoid making motion
or color the only carrier of state.
